I work on learning algorithms and executable environments for long-horizon coding agents, with an emphasis on better credit allocation, efficient search, and auditable training data.

Selected work

  • DivOPD · Rollout-aware on-policy distillationIdentified update-level credit-allocation failure in vanilla OPD, where a small number of long rollouts could dominate each learner batch. Designed a validate-cover-focus batch composer that filters invalid supervision, preserves trajectory coverage, and prioritizes turns with high learning need.
  • HBR-GSPO · Hierarchical agentic reinforcement learningDesigned a two-level denoised advantage estimator for Coordinator-SubAgent search. Candidate actions are replayed from the same state, retrieval noise is marginalized through resampling, and actions are compared with group-relative estimates. Paired finish/continue controls learn a stopping boundary without a hand-tuned length penalty.
  • LongCat2.5 · Executable data synthesisBuilt Harbor-based task environments for SWE-SQL and OfficeQA-Pro. Developed an auditable pipeline spanning document sampling, K3 question generation and solving, independent validation, programmatic answer recomputation, and structured-rubric filtering.
