Reinforcement Learning for Recommendation

Jun. 2025 — Sep. 2025Recommendation · Multi-Objective RL · Production Systems

I developed and shipped a reinforcement-learning system for personalized utility-weight tuning in WeChat Official Accounts and Channels advertising.

Selected work

  • DRL-PUT frameworkFormulated multi-objective utility-weight tuning as a deep-RL decision problem to reduce manual tuning cost and adapt to user heterogeneity and seasonal drift. Benchmarked DDPG and SAC before selecting REINFORCE for the sparse, high-variance reward setting.
  • Personalized multi-objective rewardCombined platform revenue with user-value signals including CTR, CTR30, and CVR. Campaign-specific weights supported click-through, conversion, and impression objectives, while historical CTR, CVR, and CPM informed personalized decisions.
  • Offline-to-online production stackCombined continuous-action discretization with semantically related weight grouping, compressing the action space to roughly 10³ choices and meeting real-time inference requirements at more than 100 million monthly active users.

Online results

  • +9.7%CTR in a 25% traffic A/B test
  • +7.7%CTR30 at billion-impression scale
  • +1.26%CVR with no significant core-revenue regression
